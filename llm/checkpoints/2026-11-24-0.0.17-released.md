## Restart summary

Wharfie 0.0.17 is the published preview. It is the first preview whose Hetzner
route works against the current Hetzner API, and the first backed by a
completed operational soak on AWS and Hetzner. The soak ran 48 hours, not 72.

Version 0.0.16 was tagged and built but never published: its November 5
campaign failed at the Hetzner rehearsal, and its held publication was rejected
on 2026-11-19. The `v0.0.16` tag stays where it is.

0.0.17 ships one dated known issue: an operator command that opens and closes
the local store while the resident writes can cause briefly stale reads and,
rarely, a resident exit with `MDB_PROBLEM`. It is not a regression. It is
fixed on `master` by #181 and #182 and ships in 0.0.18, which is not released.

This checkpoint records state only. It makes no product-direction change. The
priorities in [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) are unchanged.

## Known issue shipped in 0.0.17

Recorded in
[the recipient guide](../../docs/guides/recipient-preview.md#known-issue-operator-commands-while-the-service-writes)
and tracked in [issue #183](https://github.com/wharfie/wharfie/issues/183).
Operator commands (`service status`, `inspect`, `history`, `output`, `start`
and other mutations, and the same through `deployment exec`) open and close the
resident's LMDB store from another process. That is the lmdb-js
[#370](https://github.com/kriszyp/lmdb-js/issues/370) pattern, present on lmdb
3.4.4 and 3.5.11. The fix merged on `master` after the tag: #181 serves
operator reads through the resident instead of opening its live store, and
#182 keeps each local store open in one process at a time. It ships in 0.0.18.
The issue stays open until 0.0.18 is published.
